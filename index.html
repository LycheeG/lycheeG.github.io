Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Lychee's Nail and Lash Home Studio located?

We are located at Unit 69, 3028 The Boulevard, Carrara QLD 4211, on the Gold Coast. We are a cozy home studio offering a private, personalised experience with a beautiful lake view.

What are the opening hours?

We are open Sunday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We are closed on Saturdays.

How do I book an appointment?

The easiest way to book is by sending a text to 0451 329 155. You can also message us on our Facebook page. Walk-ins are welcome when available.

What nail services do you offer?

We offer classic manicures ($35), gel manicures ($50), builder gel infill/full sets, pedicures (from $40), nail art, French tips, chrome/metallic finishes, and mani-pedi combos. All services include a complimentary consultation.

How much do lash extensions cost?

Hybrid lash extensions start at $120 and volume at $140. Lash removal and cleansing is $30. Refills are free at 1 week, 50% at 2 weeks, and 80% at 3 weeks.

Do I need to bring anything to my appointment?

If you choose normal polish, please bring your own polish colour. For all other services, we provide everything. Please arrive with clean, product-free nails for best results.
